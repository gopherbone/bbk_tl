Games from Chinese pocket dictionaries, now in English.

In the 2000s, Chinese students carried electronic dictionaries that could also run games, and a whole scene of RPGs grew up on them. These are those games, running on an emulator in your browser and translated so you can play them without reading Chinese.

These translations were made with AI.

Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, did the translation and the work around it: the English font, the ROM patching tools and the test playthroughs. A human project lead directed it and made the calls on style and names. No professional translator or editor has reviewed the text, so expect some stiff phrasing and the odd mistake.

What were these?

BBK (步步高) electronic dictionaries were everyday school gear in China in the early 2000s. Models like the A4980 and A4988 had a 159×96 one-bit LCD, a keyboard and a 6502-family processor. They could also load .gam game files, and hobbyists wrote RPGs, strategy games, puzzles and ports for them, swapping them on forums. Some, like Demonbane Chronicle, also credit BBK's own game team.

The games were made for a screen smaller than a business card and a few keys, and they are surprisingly ambitious: long stories, turn-based battles, shops, side quests and save files, all in one bit per pixel.

How it works

Each game runs on an emulator of the dictionary's hardware, compiled to WebAssembly, so it behaves the way it did on the device. Text that the original drew from the dictionary's Chinese font is redrawn by a small variable-width English renderer patched into the game, and menus and pictures with Chinese text are redrawn too. Your in-game saves are kept in this browser.

Controls: the arrow keys move, OK is the dictionary's Enter key, and Back is its Exit key. On a phone, use the on-screen pad, and turn the phone sideways for a bigger layout.

If you have your own copy of a game, each translation is also available as a .bps patch. Apply it with any BPS patcher (Floating IPS, Rom Patcher JS, beat) and play the result in the gam4980 libretro core.
